Line 2 Anomaly Squad

Support

Line 2 Anomaly Squad is a quiet observation game set on a late-night subway. Walk through the car, look closely, and place a sticker on anything that doesn't belong. Get off at the station to submit what you found.

How to play

  • Tap Start inspection to board the train.
  • Look around the car. When something seems out of place, tap the screen to place a sticker at the center.
  • Get off at the station to submit what you found.
  • Open Settings on the title screen to reduce screen shake or sound.

Contact

Questions, bug reports or data requests: hesse@supercent.io. Please include your device model and the date you played.
