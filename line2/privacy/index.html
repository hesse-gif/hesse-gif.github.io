Ads and your choices

The App shows ads through Google AdMob. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads). On iPhone and iPad, you can limit ad tracking in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking and Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising. Where required by law, the App asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

Sharing

Information is shared only with the service providers listed above, for the purposes listed above. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention and deletion

Information is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above, and according to each service provider's retention settings. To ask for deletion of data related to your device, email us at the address below with the date you played; uninstalling the App stops further collection.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Children

The App is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the App, contact us and we will delete related data.
